About Me
Hi! I'm Yung Chiang, an aspiring Gameplay Programmer and Game Designer. I started tinkering with game development back when I was 12, and have been actively participating in game jams since 2024. As an undergraduate of Nanyang Technological University majoring in Information Engineering and Media, I am currently building up my experience by working on different genres of games to broaden my scope of expertise.

Keen on giving some of my projects a go? Listed below are some of the projects I had the pleasure to work on, either individually or with a group of my fellow classmates. Games in the Browser tab can be played with just a click!

DecayBound
Roguelike Deckbuilder University
A classic roguelike deckbuilder, embellised with engaging combat mechanics such as body part targeting and element combinations.

This was developed by 3 members for our "Undergraduate Research Experience on Campus" project. I was responsible for leading the team and developing the gameplay. Namely, developing a turn-based card combat with complex enemy decision-making and modular card systems.


Timeframe: 1 Year

Drifting Soul
Survivor-like Game Jam
A unique gameplay where players possess the bodies of slain foes to fight an endless horde of enemies. With their soul decaying while outside a body, players have to possess and defeat enemies to survive as long as they can.

This is a 2-member project, where I used Unity, C#, and Aseprite to design and program the gameplay, user interface, and animations. Utilising state machines, player-possessed bodies follow through actions even when unpossessed, and enemies react to player movement.


Timeframe: 10 Days
Trinity Tower
Strategy Roguelike Game Jam
A methodical experience where players select from a cast of characters, each with their own expertise, and scale a tower full of monsters and treasure. With randomly generated dungeons, each gameplay experience will be unique.

This is a individual submission for the GameCraft 2024 Game Jam, where I programmed procedurally generated dungeons. A unique combat mechanic was designed, where characters' actions are determined with input randomness from dice rolls.


Timeframe: 1 Week

Lil Quacks Battleground
Real-Time Strategy Roguelite University
A game all about strategic time management and unit manipulation. Players take control of defending against waves of enemies, collecting resources to produce controllable units.

This is a two-person project made for an undergraduate course. In the design aspect, I was responsible for game design, level design, user interface, and animations. I was also responsible for the gameplay programming, namely handling of units through Unity's Entity Component System, as well as user interaction for selecting and controlling units.


Timeframe: 4 Weeks
Ruin Dweller
Dungeon Crawler Action Game Jam
A twist on the Dungeon Crawler genre, bringing in action-style combat with time-based mechanics. A hollow soundscape, mixed with the cramped corridors and limited movement creates an unsettling ambience.

This was an individual project that I submitted for my first participation in a game jam. On this project, I familiarised myself with the tools provided by Unity and C# to programme player and enemy movement. The A* pathfinding algorithm provided an optimal solution for entity movement.




Timeframe: 9 Days
